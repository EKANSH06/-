Open when you're tired⌄

You have been carrying a lot, and you carry it quietly, so most people never see how heavy it is. I see it. Put it down for tonight.

Whatever is waiting in your inbox will still be there tomorrow, and it is almost certainly smaller than it feels right now. Drink some water. Have a cold coffee if you want one. Lie down for a bit.

Open when work is too much⌄

You study history, so you already know this: most things that felt urgent at the time turned out to be footnotes.

Pick the one thing that truly has to happen tomorrow and write it down. Everything else can wait in line. Asking for a lighter week, or taking a day off, is a normal thing people do, and you are allowed to be one of those people.

Your work does not get to decide how much you are worth. You are worth a lot, and that was settled before any job.

Ekansh

Open when you can't sleep⌄

Try the breathing circle first. Then pick a place you love and walk through it slowly in your head, one room or one street at a time.

If your mind keeps listing tomorrow's tasks, tell it they are already written down and it can stop holding them. It is late where you are and I am probably awake somewhere on the same planet, thinking about you.

Ekansh

Open when you doubt yourself⌄

The voice telling you that you are behind, or not enough, is a bad narrator. It gets the facts wrong.

The facts are that you are sharp, you are kind, and you notice what other people miss. You keep going even when you are worn out. I have watched you do it, and I think it is remarkable.
